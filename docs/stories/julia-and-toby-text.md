# Toby and Julia

## 1. Julia Meets Toby

Julia is six. She is in her backyard, playing on her scooter.

Then she hears a yelp. "Yelp! Yelp!" It is from the wood.

Julia hops up on the gate and jumps down. Then she runs to the wood.

Under a tree sits a dragon. He is white, and his scales shine pink and green and blue.

The dragon looks up. A big tear drips down his nose.

"I am Toby," he said. "I ran away from home."

"Is your home far?" asked Julia. "It is a cave on top of Super-Tall Peak," said Toby.

"But what made you run away?" asked Julia. Toby looks down at his feet. He will not say.

Julia pats his nose. "You can stay with me," she said. "I have a play house!"

Toby squeezes into the play house. He just fits. "Hush!" said Julia. "Mum must not see you."

## 2. The Bent Wheel

The next day, Julia has to go to class. "Stay in the play house," she said. "I will be back after lunch."

Toby waits and waits.

After class, Julia runs to the backyard. "Toby! I am back!"

But then she stops. Her scooter is on its side, and the wheel is bent!

"Toby, did you do this?"

Toby looks down at his feet. He is so, so sad. A big tear drips off his nose.

Julia huffs. "That was my best scooter!" She stomps off into the house.

But Julia can not stay cross for long.

Soon she is back with her kite. "Come on, Toby," she said. "Let's play."

## 3. The Painting

After class, Julia gets out her paints. "Toby, I will paint you," she said. "Sit still."

Toby sits as still as a rock. Julia paints his white scales, his tail and his big feet.

"It is the best painting!" said Julia. "Wait here. I will get a drink."

When she gets back, the paint pots are on the grass. There is paint on the play house, on Toby, and on her painting!

"Toby! My painting!"

Toby looks down. His tail droops. He is too sad to say a thing.

Julia stamps her foot. Then she sits on the back step and sulks.

But Toby looks so sad. And he has pink paint on his nose.

Julia grins. "Toby, you have a pink nose!" She wipes it off, and he grins back.

## 4. Whoa!

The next day, Julia gets back from class. Her best cup is in bits on the grass.

"Not again, Toby!" Toby just looks sad. Julia sighs.

Then one day, Julia runs back fast and peeks in at the play house.

A big bee buzzes at Toby's nose. Buzz! Buzz!

Toby gets cross. His spikes stick up.

And then her dolls zoom up, high, high, high! Her drum zooms up. Her tea set zooms up!

Crash! Bang! Smash! They all come down.

Julia's chin drops. "Whoa!"

"Toby, you did that! That is so, so cool! Do it on me! Make me float!"

Toby looks down. "I can not," he said. "I can not help it. I just get upset, and things zoom up and smash."

Julia thinks hard. Then she grins. "Then we will fix it," she said.

## 5. Float Lessons

Each day after class, Julia and Toby sneak off to the wood.

"I bet floating is like feelings," said Julia. "When you get upset, things zoom up. So let's start there."

"Toby, lift up that tin can." Toby looks at the can. He huffs. He puffs. Not a thing.

Toby gets cross. And the can zooms up, up, up, and lands in a tree!

"Now that stick," said Julia. Toby looks hard at the stick.

The stick shoots up and bonks Julia on the chin! "Ow! Toby!"

Toby droops. "I am no good at this," he said.

Day after day, it is the same. Cans zoom off. Sticks smash. Not one thing floats the right way.

## 6. Day After Day

Day six. Toby gets upset, and a log zooms into the pond. Splash!

Day nine. A branch shoots up and lands on the play house. Crunch!

Day ten. Toby sits on a log and sighs. "I can not do it," he said. "I give up."

"No, you can," said Julia. But she is fed up too.

It is dusk. Julia sits on the grass with her hand up, flat. "Just one last go," she said.

Toby is not looking at the branch. He is looking at Julia. He is not cross. He is not sad.

And then the branch lifts up. It drifts this way. It drifts that way.

It floats down, soft as a leaf, and lands in Julia's hand.

Julia looks at the branch. Toby looks at the branch. "Toby!" said Julia. "You did it!"

Toby blinks. "I did it," he said. "I did it! But how?"

## 7. Julia Floats

The next day, they rush back to the wood. "Do it again!" said Julia.

Toby looks at the branch. It lifts and drifts and lands in her hand. Then a stick. Then a tin can.

"Now me," said Julia. She stands as still as a rock.

Toby looks at Julia. And then her feet lift off the grass.

Up, up, up she floats! Higher than the gate. Higher than the play house.

"Whoa!" said Julia. "Toby, I am floating! I am floating up high!"

Toby looks up at her, and she glides this way and that. She glides past the treetops, and the birds glide with her.

She loops the loop! She spins! She dips down to the pond and zooms back up!

"This is the best day!" Julia yells. "Again! Again!"

Far away, on top of Super-Tall Peak, it is dark. In a deep cave, a big black dragon wakes up.

Its claws scrape the rock. Smoke curls from its nose.

"Toby," it hisses. "I can feel you. You can not hide from me."
