Yara Reads sound recording

All 49 sounds of American English that the sound buttons play, and 4 chunks that show as one tile. One person records all of them, so every button sounds like the same voice. It takes about ten minutes.

How to record

  1. Find a quiet room with soft things around (a bedroom is better than a kitchen). A phone's voice memo app is fine; hold it about a hand's width from your mouth.
  2. Record everything in one go, top to bottom, as one file. Leave a second of silence at the start and the end.
  3. Say pure sounds, the way they're taught in class: "sss", not "suh"; a short clipped "t", not "tuh". The note under each sound says how.
  4. Say the sounds in your own natural American voice. Don't put on an accent.
The one rule: say the sound, pause for a second, say its word, then pause for a slow count of three before the next one: sss ... sun ...   mmm ... map ... That pattern is how the recording gets cut into one file per sound. If you fumble one, pause and say that sound and word again.
